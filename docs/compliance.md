# Compliance Guide

## Core Rule

If a source, material, license, or fact cannot be verified, do not treat it as safe for publication.

## Privacy

Do not include:

- Private messages
- Account dashboards
- Personal identities
- Phone numbers, addresses, emails, or IDs
- Cookies, tokens, API keys, or credentials
- Private comments or user data
- Voice samples or likenesses without authorization

## Copyright And Licensing

Before production or publication, confirm authorization for:

- Video clips
- Images
- Fonts
- BGM
- Sound effects
- Icons
- Voice assets
- Likenesses
- Scripts and captions

Unknown status means:

`pending manual confirmation`

## Trend Research

Trend research may only use:

- Public sources
- Authorized sources
- User-provided materials
- Properly recorded source notes

Trend research must not:

- Bypass platform restrictions
- Collect private data
- Use cookies or account access without authorization
- Fabricate heat, rank, views, likes, or platform conclusions

## Hallucination And Claims

Do not write guesses as facts. Mark these as pending verification:

- Current trend rankings
- Platform policy interpretations
- Performance promises
- Legal conclusions
- License assumptions

## Publication Reminder

This project assists planning and review. It does not guarantee that a generated script, plan, or video is lawful, platform-compliant, publishable, or effective.
