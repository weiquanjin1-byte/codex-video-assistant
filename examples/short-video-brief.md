# Short Video Brief Example

## Topic

How ordinary people can use AI to improve learning efficiency.

## Platform

Douyin / Xiaohongshu style short video.

## Duration

Under 60 seconds.

## Audience

AI beginners, students, and everyday learners who want practical study habits.

## Style

Clear, practical, rhythmic, and helpful. Avoid exaggerated marketing.

## Core Point

AI is most useful when it helps you break down goals, explain difficult ideas, practice recall, and review progress.

## Compliance Notes

- Use self-made visuals or authorized materials.
- Do not show private chat logs.
- Do not claim guaranteed learning results.
- Confirm BGM, font, icon, and voice rights before publishing.
