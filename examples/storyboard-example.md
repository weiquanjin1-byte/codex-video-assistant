# Storyboard Example

| Time | Voiceover | Visual | Caption | Editing Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | AI will not magically make you disciplined. | Clean title card with study desk illustration or self-made graphic | AI is not magic | Quick title reveal |
| 3-8s | But it can make every study session easier to start. | Checklist graphic: goal, explain, quiz, review | Easier to start | Smooth cut |
| 8-16s | Start with one clear goal. | Text card: "What do I need in 30 minutes?" | Set one goal | Hold for readability |
| 16-28s | Ask AI to explain in three levels. | Three stacked cards: beginner, example, mistake | Three-level explanation | Step transition |
| 28-40s | Turn the answer into five recall questions. | Quiz card animation or static checklist | Test yourself | Slightly faster rhythm |
| 40-51s | Ask it to explain only what you missed. | Highlighted gap in notes | Fix weak points | Calm pacing |
| 51-60s | Use AI to organize thinking, not replace it. | Final summary card | Organize thinking | End cleanly |

## Asset Notes

Use self-made graphics, authorized stock, or simple text cards. Do not use private screenshots or unlicensed icons.
